Mobility-Aware and Energy-Efficient Fog Computing 
Consider a smart transportation environment in which vehicles and mobile users continuously 
move between different fog-node coverage areas. Tasks assigned to a fog node may therefore 
experience disconnection, increased latency, or the need for migration. Evaluate the 
challenges of mobility support in Fog Computing and design a mobility-aware, energy
efficient computing framework. 
Develop an architecture incorporating mobile devices/vehicles, fog nodes, mobility prediction, 
task migration, resource allocation, and cloud support. Evaluate different strategies for task 
migration, fog-node selection, energy consumption, latency, and service continuity. Justify
